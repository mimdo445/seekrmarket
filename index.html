Simple from the start

Three steps. A clearer decision.

Seekr starts with buyer intent, routes the request to relevant sellers and keeps offers together so the buyer can choose with confidence.

01

Post what you need

Choose a category, describe the item or job, add a budget and timing, then share your general area.

02

Receive structured offers

Matching sellers reply with price, condition, availability and pickup or service details. No store catalogue is required.

03

Compare and complete

Review the options, select the right fit and—when live payments are enabled—complete checkout and handoff in Seekr.
